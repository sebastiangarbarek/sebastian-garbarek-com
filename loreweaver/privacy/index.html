Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 25, 2026

Introduction

This privacy policy explains how we handle information when you use our iOS application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

Information We Collect

Loreweaver does not collect, store, or track any personal information about you. We do not use analytics, tracking tools, or any data collection mechanisms.

Third-Party AI Services

Loreweaver uses Google Gemini and OpenAI to provide text and image generation features. When you use these features, your prompts and requests are sent directly to these third-party services for processing.

The data you submit is processed by these services in accordance with their own privacy policies:

Google Gemini Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

OpenAI Privacy Policy: https://openai.com/privacy

We do not store copies of your prompts, generated content, or any data sent to or received from these services. All processing is done in real-time, and no data persists on our servers.

Data Storage

Loreweaver does not maintain any servers or databases that store user information. We do not create user accounts, and we do not retain any data after you close the app.

Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. The app does not require users to provide any personal information.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new privacy policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us.
